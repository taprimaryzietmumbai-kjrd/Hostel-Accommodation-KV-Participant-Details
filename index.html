💳 Payment Details Calculator

If the participant occupies the hostel, hostel payment is calculated automatically from the Actual Check-in Date and Time and Actual Date and Time of Checking Out entered in the Participant Arrival Details tab. Hostel billing is based on actual Check-in and Check-out time: every completed 24-hour period counts as 1 day, and any part of an additional 24-hour period counts as another full day. For example, 12 noon to 12 noon next day = 1 day; 12 noon to 2 PM next day = 2 days.
